About Our Company

North America's Leading B2C + B2B Show Organizer

Marketplace Events creates vibrant expositions connecting enthusiasts with experts, products and services in dynamic face-to-face environments.

The company produces more than 135 business-to-consumer and trade shows in North America, including 67 home and garden shows, 23 holiday shopping shows, 16 sport and outdoor shows, 10 travel shows and 22 regional trade shows.

Across 60 markets, these events attract more than 35,000 exhibitors, over 2.3 million attendees and another 6 million unique web visitors annually. Marketplace Events produces some of the most successful and longest-running shows in North America, including market-leading shows in Seattle, Minneapolis, Indianapolis, Vancouver, Calgary and Montreal—some of which have thrived in their markets for more than 75 years.

About the show

Inspiration starts here.

The BC Home + Garden Show is a vibrant marketplace where homeowners can meet real people, discover products and services, explore inspiring displays and learn from experienced home professionals.

Whether you arrive with a renovation plan or simply want fresh ideas, the show brings together a wide range of home-related experts and products. Explore 400+ booths, discover unique feature displays and get practical advice for your next project.

From kitchens and bathrooms to flooring, design and outdoor living, find inspiration and make valuable connections in one convenient place.
